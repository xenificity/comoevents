Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No price, no ads, no in-app purchases. It's a community project made in Columbia.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. Sign in with Apple only keeps your saved events and interests in your own iCloud so they follow you to a new phone.

An event is wrong or missing.

Events come straight from the public sources above and refresh every couple of hours. If a venue is missing or a pin is in the wrong place, email xenificity@icloud.com or open an issue on GitHub.

Android?

iPhone first. The event feed is open, so an Android or web version is possible later.

Can I add my own event?

Not in the app yet. Post it on Eventbrite, AllEvents or Vox's community calendar and it will show up within a few hours.
