Things to do in Columbia, MO this weekend: October 30–November 1, 2026

It is Halloween season in Columbia, and this weekend's calendar shows it. From Friday, October 30 through Sunday, November 1, CoMo Events lists 148 public events around town, 13 of them free.

Friday night, start with Superheroes & Heroes Dinner at Park Restaurant at Park Restaurant & Bar. On Saturday, A Spooktacular Symphony: Halloween with MOSY at Missouri Theatre is our top pick. Mizzou fans: Mizzou Soccer vs. LSU is on Sunday.

With kids, there are 16 family events to choose from, such as Weird Kids' School's Out Club. Night owls have 30 concerts, parties and nightlife events. See every Halloween event on our Halloween page.
