Things to do in Columbia, MO this weekend: November 13–15, 2026

It is Thanksgiving season in Columbia, and this weekend's calendar shows it. From Friday, November 13 through Sunday, November 15, CoMo Events lists 137 public events around town, 26 of them free.

Friday night, start with Party With the Stars at Columbia Public Library. On Saturday, Columbia Farmers Market at Winter Season is our top pick. Sunday closes things out with Columbia Coffee Club | Foodies + New Friends at Cafe of the Month.

With kids, there are 10 family events to choose from, such as Annual Christmas Bazaar. Night owls have 16 concerts, parties and nightlife events. See every Thanksgiving event on our Thanksgiving page.
