Every week we go through every public event in Columbia, Missouri, from The Blue Note and Mizzou to the library and the farmers market, and pick the ten worth planning your weekend around. A new guide goes up every Monday and fills in as the week goes on.

This weekend: November 20–22, 2026

It is Thanksgiving season in Columbia, and this weekend's calendar shows it. From Friday, November 20 through Sunday, November 22, CoMo Events lists 118 public events around town, 15 of them free.

Friday night, start with 5th Annual Merry Moments with Santa & Mrs. Claus at Orr Street Studios. On Saturday, Columbia Farmers Market at Winter Season is our top pick. Sunday closes things out with Intergenerational Rock Band Choir at The Blue Note. Mizzou fans: Mizzou Men’s Basketball vs. Le Moyne is on Friday.

With kids, there are 15 family events to choose from, such as Lend & Learn Play Time. Night owls have 19 concerts, parties and nightlife events. See every Thanksgiving event on our Thanksgiving page.
