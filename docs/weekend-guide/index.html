Every week we go through every public event in Columbia, Missouri, from The Blue Note and Mizzou to the library and the farmers market, and pick the ten worth planning your weekend around. A new guide goes up every Monday and fills in as the week goes on.

This weekend: November 20–22, 2026

It is Thanksgiving season in Columbia, and this weekend's calendar shows it. From Friday, November 20 through Sunday, November 22, CoMo Events lists 157 public events around town, 17 of them free.

Friday night, start with Craft Club at Ladybird Cafe. On Saturday, Harvest of Gratitude Community Dinner at St Paul AME Church is our top pick. Mizzou fans: Missouri Tigers vs. Kentucky Wildcats at Faurot Field at Memorial Stadium is on Saturday.

With kids, there are 16 family events to choose from, such as Lend & Learn Play Time. Night owls have 26 concerts, parties and nightlife events. See every Thanksgiving event on our Thanksgiving page.
