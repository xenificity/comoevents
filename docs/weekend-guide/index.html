Weekend guide: the best things to do in Columbia, MO

Every week we go through every public event in Columbia, Missouri, from The Blue Note and Mizzou to the library and the farmers market, and pick the ten worth planning your weekend around. A new guide goes up every Monday and fills in as the week goes on.

This weekend: November 13–15, 2026

It is Thanksgiving season in Columbia, and this weekend's calendar shows it. From Friday, November 13 through Sunday, November 15, CoMo Events lists 159 public events around town, 26 of them free.

Friday night, start with Party With the Stars at Columbia Public Library. On Saturday, Columbia Farmers Market at Winter Season is our top pick. Sunday closes things out with Columbia Coffee Club | Foodies + New Friends at Cafe of the Month.

With kids, there are 10 family events to choose from, such as Annual Christmas Bazaar. Night owls have 17 concerts, parties and nightlife events. See every Thanksgiving event on our Thanksgiving page.
