Hinkebein & Watson Concert

🗓️Thu, Dec 10 · 6:30 PM – 7:30 PM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

Columbia roots music veterans Joe Hinkebein and Robert Watson will bring their decades of synergy to the library tonight. This dynamic duo delivers a master class in traditional old-time and bluegrass music. Expect soulful vocal harmonies and intricate instrumental duets blending fiddle, mandolin, guitar and clawhammer banjo. Don't miss an evening of authentic acoustic folk. Adults.
