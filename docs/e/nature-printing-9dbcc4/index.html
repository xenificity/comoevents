Nature Printing

🗓️Mon, Nov 16 · 3 PM – 4:30 PM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

Learn how to make simple, yet stunning prints using found botanical and mineral specimens! We'll have some curated natural items for you to use, but feel free to bring your own leaves, flowers, rocks, shells, seeds, etc. (Tip: the fresher the item, the better the print!) This session is designed for people with low-sensory needs. Adults and teens. Registration not required, but space is limited.
