College of Education & Human Development First-Gen Fill-Up

🗓️Thu, Nov 5 · 9 AM – 11:30 PM CST
📍Townsend Hall Lobby, 611 Conley Ave, Columbia, MO
👤First-Generation Student Initiatives

Join the College of Education & Human Development community in celebrating National First-Generation Celebration Week with coffee and breakfast refreshments as well as activity stations in Townsend Hall Lobby. Come by to write a note of thanks to families or mentors acknowledging their support, take a photo or design a button to share "Why I'm First" or "Proud to be First," and share and/or read notes of advice of First-Gen alumni, professors, staff and peers who walked the same path.
