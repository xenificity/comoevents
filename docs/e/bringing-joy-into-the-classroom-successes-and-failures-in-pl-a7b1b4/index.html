Bringing Joy into the Classroom: Successes and Failures in Playful Teaching in Large Lecture Courses

🗓️Tue, Nov 3 · 9:30 AM – 10:30 AM CST
📍Teaching for Learning Center
👤Teaching For Learning Center

This session explores practical strategies for fostering joy, connection, and active participation in large lecture courses. Participants will learn how hands-on activities, iClicker, place-based and experiential learning, multimedia resources, and a culture of grace can help re-engage students, reduce anxiety, and support meaningful learning.
