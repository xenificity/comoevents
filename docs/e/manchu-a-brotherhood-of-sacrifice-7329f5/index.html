"Manchu: A Brotherhood of Sacrifice"

🗓️Wed, Nov 11 · 6 PM – 8:30 PM CST
📍Ragtag Cinema · 10 Hitt St., Columbia, 10 Hitt St, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

Join us for a free screening of "Manchu: A Brotherhood of Sacrifice," a documentary by Matthew Wilcox exploring the history of America’s involvement in Vietnam as told by veterans of the U.S. Army Manchus who served in-country 1966-70. Panel discussion to follow. (116 min., TV-14) Adults and teens 14 or older. Pick up free tickets starting at noon the day of the show at Ragtag or at the library.
