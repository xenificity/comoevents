Saturday Morning Science: Because Chat Said So

🗓️Sat, Dec 5 · 10 AM – 12 PM CST
📍1201 Rollins Street Columbia, MO 65201
👤Visit Columbia

Massively popular generated-AI tools are changing what we know and how we know. They are often powerful tools for gaining knowledge, and in many other instances, technologies that can be used to manipulate and misinform people. Fake images, video, and audio are becoming popular tools to either mislead or simply to make political points. This presentation will explore what we know and how we know in the AI era.
