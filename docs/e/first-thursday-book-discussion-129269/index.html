First Thursday Book Discussion

🗓️Thu, Dec 3 · 12 PM – 1 PM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO

In her debut novel, "Maud's Line," Margaret Verble, herself an enrolled citizen of the Cherokee Nation, does a beautiful job of vividly describing a sometimes hardscrabble Indigenous life that nevertheless has the comfort that familiarity and extended family bring. Join us to discuss this finalist for the Pulitzer Prize. Adults.
