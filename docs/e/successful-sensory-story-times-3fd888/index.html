Successful Sensory Story Times

🗓️Wed, Nov 4 · 6:30 PM – 7:30 PM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

This workshop for early childhood educators and professionals is presented by Sherry Norfolk, co-author of "Storytelling Strategies for Reaching and Teaching Children with Special Needs." This highly interactive workshop will address why sensory story times are important, what a sensory story time is and how to present them effectively. Adults. DHSS-SCCR approved, 1 clock hour. Please register.
