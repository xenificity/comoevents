“Hearing Route 66: the Songs and Sounds of the Mother Road,” talk with Dr. Murph

🗓️Fri, Nov 13 · 3 PM CST
📍Stewart Hall, Stewart Hall, University of Missouri, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Music

In celebration of the 100th anniversary of Route 66, Dr. Murph will present a lecture exploring the songs and sounds that have shaped the highway's rich history in Missouri. This event is brought to you by Hearing Place StudioLab (via the Center for the Humanities), the State Historical Society of Missouri, and the Budds Center for American Music Studies.
