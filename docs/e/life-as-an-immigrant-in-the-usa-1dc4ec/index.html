Life as an Immigrant in the USA

🗓️Wed, Oct 28 · 6 PM – 7 PM CDT
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

In honor of National Immigrants Day, hear firsthand the story of medical practitioner Ralph Abalo, from the day he moved to the U.S. from Togo, West Africa, to now, working as a clinical perfusionist. Learn about his challenges around language, cultural differences, employment and education, as well as his efforts to provide educational opportunity to the children of Togo. Adults and teens.
