Graduates on Faurot Field

🗓️Fri, Nov 13 · 2 PM – 4 PM CST
📍Faurot Field, Enter at South East Field Gates, 600 E. Stadium Blvd., Columbia, MO 65201, United States

Celebrate your upcoming graduation with a free, professional photo on Faurot Field! Student Affairs and Mizzou Athletics invite all December graduates to Graduates on Faurot—an exclusive, free event honoring your achievement. Step onto the turf and let a professional photographer capture your Mizzou moment. Wear your best Black & Gold, whether that's your official cap and gown or your favorite game day outfit. Important guidelines: This event is invite-only, strictly for December Mizzou graduates. Guests or outside visitors are not permitted to attend with you. No food or drinks. No bags.…
