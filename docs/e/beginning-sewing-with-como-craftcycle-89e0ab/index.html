Beginning Sewing With Como Craftcycle

🗓️Sun, Nov 22 · 1:30 PM – 3:30 PM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

At these classes, make an easy shirt using your own measurements for a perfect fit. Learn to draft a pattern, highlight a favorite fabric and sew a finished, unique shirt. This class is for beginners who know how to sew a straight seam on a sewing machine. Most supplies provided; bring a sewing machine if you have one. Plan to attend all four classes. Adults only. Please register starting Oct. 19.
