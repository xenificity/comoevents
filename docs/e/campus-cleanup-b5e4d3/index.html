Campus Cleanup

🗓️Sat, Nov 14 · 10 AM – 12 PM CST
📍Carnahan Quad, Carnahan Quad, University of Missouri, Columbia, MO
👤Sustain Mizzou

Our Stream Team will be hosting a clean up across campus on November 14 from 10am - 12. This service event will focus on areas on campus that tend to be litter-heavy, particularly after Halloween festivities. Volunteers will sort the trash from recycling and count the bags to track impact. Come enjoy the fall weather and make a difference on campus! We will meet on Carnahan Quad, south of Traditions Plaza, where a few tents and tables will be set up. Gloves and trash bags will be provided.
