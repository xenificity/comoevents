The Boone’s Lick Road

🗓️Sat, Nov 14 · 1 PM – 3 PM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

Join the Boone’s Lick Road Association for a presentation by genealogist and public historian Dorris Keeven-Franke. Learn the important history of the "Big Road," those who traveled it and those who built towns along it, and how it contributed to our culture and heritage. See how the route changed according to society’s needs and what remains today of our state’s first major roadway. Adults.
