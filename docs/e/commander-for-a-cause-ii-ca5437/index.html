Commander for a Cause II

🗓️Sat, Nov 28, 12 PM – Sun, Nov 29, 12 AM CST
📍Hexagon Alley, 111 S 9th St, Columbia, MO 65201
👤Hexagon Alley

Commander for a Cause is a 24-Hour event where entry supports City of Refuge. Come and play Commander with us, and enjoy prizing, good people and good times. This year Hexagon Alley is partnering with Mageling's Games on this awesome event, and will be hosting the second half of the day - from noon to midnight. This year, we're not JUST doing Commander, we will have several other games such as a special Magic Repack Draft, Riftbound, Blood on the Clocktower, and other games as well. Will you be there?
