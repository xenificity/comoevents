Introduction to ArcGIS Pro is a hands-on workshop designed for University of Missouri System graduate students and faculty who are new to Geographic Information Systems (GIS) or looking to build foundational skills in Esri's ArcGIS Pro software. This training provides practical experience with core GIS concepts and tools to support research and teaching. Participants will explore the ArcGIS Pro interface and learn how to create, manage, and visualize spatial data.
