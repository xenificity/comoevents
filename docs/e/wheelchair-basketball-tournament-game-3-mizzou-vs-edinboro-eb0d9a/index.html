Wheelchair Basketball Tournament - Game #3: Mizzou vs. Edinboro

🗓️Sat, Dec 5 · 9 AM – 10:30 AM CST
📍MizzouRec, 811 Hitt Street, Columbia, MO 65211, United States
👤MizzouRec

Get ready for an electric time on Ron Lykins Court as Mizzou Wheelchair Basketball hosts PennWest Edinboro! Come experience the fast-paced action of Mizzou Wheelchair Basketball on their home court, Brewer Court #10 at MizzouRec. Admission is free, so bring your friends and pack the stands. Entering is easy: MizzouRec members can come through the main entrance and tap in at the turnstiles, while guests and visitors without a membership should enter through the North Event Entrance on Rollins Street, right across from the Student Center.
