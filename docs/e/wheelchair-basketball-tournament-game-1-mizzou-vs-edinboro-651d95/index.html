Wheelchair Basketball Tournament - Game #1: Mizzou vs. Edinboro

🗓️Fri, Dec 4 · 4 PM – 5:30 PM CST
📍MizzouRec, 811 Hitt Street, Columbia, MO 65211, United States
👤MizzouRec

Get ready for an electric night on Ron Lykins Court as Mizzou Wheelchair Basketball hosts PennWest Edinboro! Come experience the fast-paced action of Mizzou Wheelchair Basketball on their home court, Brewer Court #10 at MizzouRec. Admission is free, so bring your friends and pack the stands. Entering is easy: MizzouRec members can come through the main entrance and tap in at the turnstiles, while guests and visitors without a membership should enter through the North Event Entrance on Rollins Street, right across from the Student Center.
