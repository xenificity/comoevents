Indianite 2026

🗓️Sat, Oct 31 · 5 PM – 7 PM CDT
📍Jesse Hall, University of Missouri, COLUMBIA, MO 65211, United States
👤Cultural Association of India

The Cultural Association of India presents Indianite, our 34th Annual India Night and Mizzou’s largest student-organized cultural showcase. This year’s theme is Indian Music. Join us for a two-hour production at Jesse Hall featuring Bollywood, folk, and classical dance, live vocalists, East-meets-West fusion music, a fashion show of regional Indian and Bollywood-era clothing, and storytelling woven through the night.
