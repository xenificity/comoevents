Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation @ MUFPA

🗓️Mon, Nov 30 · 5 PM – 6 PM CST
📍102 Stewart Hall (Parker Auditorium), Stewart Hall, University of Missouri, Columbia, MO
👤MU Future Providers of America

Join MU Future Providers of America on Monday, November 30th, in Stewart Hall 102 (Parker Auditorium) to hear from Dr. Dave Arnold. Dr. Arnold, MD, is a Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation Physician with an emphasis in the area of neuromuscular diseases and treatment. He received his education at the University of Louisville, with an internship in Internal Medicine and residency in Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation.
