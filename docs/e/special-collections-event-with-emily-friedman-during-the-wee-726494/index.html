The Week Of Writing is a weeklong celebration of writing in all of its forms, stages, uses, functions, approaches, and levels of joy (and challenge). We are interested in hearing how undergraduate students, graduate students, post-docs, faculty and staff use, grapple with and/or celebrate writing across campus. From the Humanities to STEM, in any language or form, writing is an act that unites us all in academia (and maybe as people?).
