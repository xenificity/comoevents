Intro to ArcGIS Online Workshop UMSystem Graduate Students and Faculty

🗓️Wed, Dec 9 · 1 PM – 5 PM CST
📍Strickland Hall, Strickland Hall, University of Missouri, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Msdis

Introduction to ArcGIS Online is a hands-on workshop for University of Missouri System graduate students and faculty who are new to ArcGIS Online or looking to strengthen their web-based GIS skills. Participants will gain practical experience with Esri's cloud-based GIS platform and learn core concepts for creating, managing, analyzing, and sharing geographic information to support research and teaching.
