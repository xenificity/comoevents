Booneslick Trail Quilters’ Guild: 2026 Quilt Show (Day 1)

🗓️Fri, Oct 23 · 9 AM – 5 PM CDT
📍3301 West Broadway Columbia, MO Columbia, MO 65203
👤Visit Columbia

Dates/Times: Friday October 23, 2026 9 am to 5 pm Saturday October 24, 2026 9 am to 4 pm (note: Show ends one hour earlier on Saturday) The show will feature over 150 quilts submitted by BTQG Guild members plus Member Challenge Quilt Entries.
