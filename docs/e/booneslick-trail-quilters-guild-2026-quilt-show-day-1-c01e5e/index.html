Booneslick Trail Quilters’ Guild: 2026 Quilt Show (Day 1)

🗓️Fri, Oct 23 · 9 AM – 5 PM CDT
📍3301 West Broadway Columbia, MO Columbia, MO 65203
👤Visit Columbia

Dates/Times: Friday October 23, 2026 9 am to 5 pm Saturday October 24, 2026 9 am to 4 pm (note: Show ends one hour earlier on Saturday) The show will feature over 150 quilts submitted by BTQG Guild members plus Member Challenge Quilt Entries. There will be free presentations, workshops and demonstrations throughout the two-day event. The Guild hosts a Silent Auction and a Marketplace where attendees can purchase handmade items created by Guild members. Two Quilts handmade by BTQG Guild members will be raffled off at the end of the show. $5 entry fee.
