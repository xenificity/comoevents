Berry Bramble Cultivation

🗓️Wed, Nov 11 · 6 PM – 7:30 PM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO

Learn how to care for and cultivate healthy berry brambles, such as blackberries, raspberries and elderberries! Once you establish these yummy perennials, you can enjoy them year after year.
