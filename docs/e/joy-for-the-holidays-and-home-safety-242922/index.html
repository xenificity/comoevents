Joy for the Holidays and Home Safety

🗓️Wed, Nov 18 · 12 PM – 1 PM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

As dementia progresses, travel can become unsafe or impractical, turning a long-anticipated visit into a source of stress for everyone involved. This session covers how to recognize when staying home is the better option, and how to make travel safe, comfortable and enjoyable for those who do choose to travel. Presented by the Alzheimer’s Association Missouri Chapter. Adults.
