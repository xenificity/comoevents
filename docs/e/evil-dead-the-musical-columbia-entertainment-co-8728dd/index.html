Evil Dead: The Musical – Columbia Entertainment Co.

🗓️Fri, Oct 30 · 7:30 PM – 9 PM CDT
📍1800 Nelwood Drive, Columbia, MO
👤Visit Columbia

You don’t need to be a fan of Evil Dead to love this show. You don’t need to be a fan of horror to love this show. You don’t even need to be a fan of musicals to love this show. As long as you like having fun… this show is for you. Is it scary? No! Is it gory? No. This is a pure comedy start to finish. It has fun with the horror genre, and in doing so appeals to a wider audience than you could ever imagine.
