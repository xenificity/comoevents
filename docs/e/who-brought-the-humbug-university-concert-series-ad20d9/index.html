Who Brought the Humbug? – University Concert Series

🗓️Sun, Dec 13 · 3 PM – 5 PM CST
📍University Concert Series, 203 S 9th St, Columbia, MO
🎟️45-$55
👤Visit Columbia

The show takes place at an ugly sweater party filled with joyful singers, incredible tap dancers , and high-energy musicians. The interactive fun kicks off with a sleigh ride of swingin’ holiday mash-ups. Things get wild when Santa raps to Rudolph over horn-band funk, Frosty tap dances to a groove, and the reindeer bust out hip-hop choreography— an unforgettable combination! The party takes a hilarious twist when two singers-turned-investigators steal the spotlight with a beatboxing version of “The Grinch” and reveal that someone brought a “HUMBUG!”
