Build Your Course AI Assistant: A Design Studio (Session 2: Build the Initial Assistant)

🗓️Tue, Nov 3 · 3 PM – 4 PM CST
📍Teaching for Learning Center
👤Teaching For Learning Center

Move beyond experimenting with AI and create a practical tool for your teaching. In this four-session, hands-on program, faculty, instructors, and graduate students with teaching responsibilities will design and build a course-specific AI assistant that addresses a meaningful instructional need. Examples include assistants that answer common course questions, explain challenging concepts, support student practice, guide problem-solving, or help students navigate course materials.
