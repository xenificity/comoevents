General Meeting

🗓️Tue, Nov 10 · 5:30 PM – 6:30 PM CST
📍Memorial Union, Gus T. Ridgel Room (S204), University of Missouri, Columbia, Missouri, United States
👤Mizzou Digital Agriculture Club

The Mizzou Digital Ag Club invites students to its First General Meeting to explore the exciting intersection of agriculture and emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence, drones, robotics, sensors, and precision agriculture. The event will introduce the club's goals, upcoming workshops, hands-on learning opportunities, and ways for students to get involved. Students from all majors are welcome, and no prior experience is required. Refreshments will be provided.
