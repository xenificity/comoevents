Mizzou PAL-OOZA Story Time

🗓️Sat, Oct 24 · 11 AM – 12 PM CDT
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

Trained members of Mizzou PALS will read with small groups of children and parents in four 15-minute sessions, so you can drop by any time. When you arrive, your child may pick a reading group to join; they'll hear books designed to improve reading comprehension and vocabulary. Different books will be shared each session, so you can attend more than one if space allows. Ages 3-5 with an adult.
