Nature Printing

🗓️Mon, Nov 16 · 6 PM – 7:30 PM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

Learn how to make simple, yet stunning prints using found botanical and mineral specimens! We'll have a supply of curated natural items for you to choose from, but feel free to bring your own leaves, flowers, rocks, shells, seeds, etc. (Tip: the fresher the item, the better the print!) Adults and teens. Registration is not required, but space is limited.
