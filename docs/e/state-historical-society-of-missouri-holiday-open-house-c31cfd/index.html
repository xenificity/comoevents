State Historical Society of Missouri Holiday Open House

🗓️Sat, Dec 5 · 9 AM – 2 PM CST
📍Center for Missouri Studies, 605 Elm St Columbia, MO 65201
👤Visit Columbia

Join the State Historical Society of Missouri and Richard Bookstore (inside the Center for Missouri Studies) for our annual community tradition featuring unique local vendors, holiday shopping, live caroling, and a visit from Santa and the Grinch! The Holiday Open House brings together makers, artisans, and small businesses for a cheerful morning of shopping and festivities. It’s the perfect place to enjoy the holiday spirit and find one-of-a-kind gifts and books for friends and family.
