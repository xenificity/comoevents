Columbia Ballet Academy Fall Festival

🗓️Sat, Oct 24 · 2 PM – 5 PM CDT
📍110 Orr St, Columbia, MO Columbia, MO 65201
👤Visit Columbia

Join Columbia Ballet Academy for a fun-filled fall celebration designed for the whole family! Our Fall Festival will have all the excitement of a carnival , with a variety of games where you can test your skills, win prizes, and enjoy some friendly competition. Little ones and families can get creative with pumpkin decorating , show off their favorite costumes during our Costume Parade , and enjoy even more fall-themed activities throughout the event. Best of all, admission is FREE!
