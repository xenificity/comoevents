Columbia Ballet Academy Fall Festival

🗓️Sat, Oct 24 · 2 PM – 5 PM CDT

Join Columbia Ballet Academy for a fun-filled fall celebration designed for the whole family! Our Fall Festival will have all the excitement of a carnival , with a variety of games where you can test your skills, win prizes, and enjoy some friendly competition. Best of all, admission is FREE!
