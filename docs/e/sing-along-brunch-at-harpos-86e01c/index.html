Sing-Along Brunch at Harpo’s

🗓️Sat, Oct 24 · 10 AM – 2 PM CDT
📍Harpo’s, 29 South 10th Street Columbia, MO 65201
👤Visit Columbia

$10 ticket purchases in advance or $15 dollars at the door gets you into the Big Brothers Big Sisters of Central Missouri’s first-ever Sing-Along Brunch, hosted by Harpo’s! Enjoy a complimentary cocktail and sing along with the crowd and emcee as we make our way through all your favorite hits—from Mamma Mia and Journey to Taylor Swift and whatever jams the audience demands! Want to make a morning of it? Order brunch or bottomless mimosas and get ready to sing, sip, and have a whole lot of fun—all while supporting Big Brothers Big Sisters of Central Missouri!
