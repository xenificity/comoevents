Eurydice – Stephens College Performing Arts

🗓️Sat, Oct 31 · 2 PM – 4 PM CDT
📍Stephens College Performing Arts, 100 Willis Ave, Columbia, MO
👤Visit Columbia

By Sarah Ruhl Eurydice reimagines the classic Greek myth of Orpheus and Eurydice from Eurydice’s perspective. After falling deeply in love with Orpheus, a gifted musician, Eurydice dies unexpectedly on her wedding day and journeys to the Underworld. There, she reunites with her father, who has been waiting for her and helps her rediscover her memories and sense of self. As Eurydice adjusts to the strange rules of the Underworld, she struggles between her love for Orpheus and her growing connection to her father. The play explores themes of love, loss, memory, and the difficulty of letting go.…
