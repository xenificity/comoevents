Holiday Open House – Presented by State Historical Society of Missouri

🗓️Sat, Dec 5 · 9 AM – 2 PM CST
📍Presented by State Historical Society of Missouri, 605 Elm St. Columbia, MO Columbia, MO 65201

Join the State Historical Society of Missouri and Richard Bookstore (inside the Center for Missouri Studies) for our annual community tradition featuring unique local vendors, holiday shopping, live caroling, and a visit from Santa and the Grinch! The Holiday Open House brings together makers, artisans, and small businesses for a cheerful morning of shopping and festivities.
