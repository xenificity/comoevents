Party With the Stars

🗓️Fri, Nov 13 · 7 PM – 9 PM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

Tonight, the distant planet Uranus joins golden Saturn in our skies! Saturn glows gold while Uranus shines aquamarine. Weather permitting, view both through a large telescope. We'll begin indoors with a brief slideshow, then head outside if skies are clear. Hosted by Val Germann, Central Missouri Astronomical Association. Enter at west end of main parking lot. Ages 10–adult. Please register.
