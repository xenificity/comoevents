Native American Storytelling With Suzanne Jones

🗓️Thu, Nov 19 · 6 PM – 7 PM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

Talking animals, mysterious beings, clever characters and encounters with the supernatural — traditional Native American stories are filled with the unexpected. Join Suzanne Jones, a tribal member or descendant of the Choctaw, Cherokee and Delaware Nations/tribes to hear traditional stories in celebration of Native American Heritage Month. Adults and families with school-aged children.
