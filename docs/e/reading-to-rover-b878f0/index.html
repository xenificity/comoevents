Reading to Rover

🗓️Mon, Dec 14 · 5:30 PM – 7 PM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

Children will read for 15 minutes to one of Ann Gafke's Teacher's Pet Therapy Dogs while a handler oversees them. It's a fun way to improve children's literacy skills. All dogs are certified therapy dogs. Please note, depending on attendance, your family may need to wait for a dog. Kindergarten age and older.
