Join Behind the Beat for a hands-on service event where a small act of kindness can make a meaningful difference in someone’s recovery journey! We’ll be creating handmade cards filled with encouragement, positivity, and support for patients at Boone Hospital and Rusk Rehabilitation Hospital. Recovery after a cardiac event, stroke, or other serious health condition can be challenging, and a simple reminder that someone cares can help patients and their families feel supported beyond their diagnosis.
