Rags to Riches: Repurposing Worn Clothes Into Crocheted Rugs

🗓️Thu, Nov 12 · 6 PM – 7:30 PM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

Learn how to turn old t-shirts, socks and other clothes ready for the rag box into skeins of cloth "yarn" and then crochet them into rugs. Both the cloth strips and regular yarn will be used to crochet the rug. Basic crochet skills are a prerequisite for this class. Some supplies provided. Bring some clean used clothes to include in your rug. Adults & teens. Please register starting October 29.
