👤Sustain Mizzou

Sustain Mizzou is hosting its' bi-annual clothing drive from Nov 2 until Wednesday, November 11th. Drop off your unwanted clothing in these bins at various locations including the Student Center and many residence halls (Hudson/Gillett, Values, Hawthorn, Southwest, Mark Twain, and more! ) These clothing donations will be available at our Clothing Swap Thursday, November 12, where you can get them free of charge.
