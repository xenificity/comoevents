Film & Feast: A Magical Evening at The Tiger Hotel

🗓️Wed, Dec 23 · 5:30 PM – 8 PM CST
📍The Tiger Hotel, 23 S 8th St, Columbia, MO 65201 Columbia, MO 65201
👤Visit Columbia

Calling all wizards and witches! Join us for a one-of-a-kind movie night watching & eating our way through our first year at wizarding school! As we watch the film, we’ll enjoy the dishes, drinks, and treats featured throughout the story, right as they appear on screen! Dinner includes 11th birthday cake, soup from the wizarding pub, the Great Hall feast, frog-shaped chocolates, and more! A full cash bar will also be available as well as butterbrew and pumpkin juice.
