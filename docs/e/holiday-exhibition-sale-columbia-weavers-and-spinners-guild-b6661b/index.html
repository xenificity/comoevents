Holiday Exhibition & Sale – Columbia Weavers and Spinners Guild

🗓️Sat, Nov 14 · 10 AM – 4 PM CST
📍3669 Legion Ln, Columbia, MO 65201 Columbia, MO 65201
👤Visit Columbia

All are welcome to come to the FREE Columbia Weavers and Spinners’ Guild Holiday Exhibition and Sale. This two day event will have demonstrations of weaving, spinning wool, needle felting, knitting, crochet, small looms, basket making and book making. Juried artists will share their work for sale as well. This is a wonderful opportunity to purchase a hand made piece of wearable art, creative one-of-a-kind houseware or unique jewelry piece.
