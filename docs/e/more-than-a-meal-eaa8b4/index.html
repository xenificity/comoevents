More Than A Meal

🗓️Thu, Nov 5, 8 AM – Thu, Nov 19, 8 AM CST
📍All MU Housing Residence Halls and Apartments, All MU Housing Residence Halls and Apartments, University of Missouri, Columbia, MO
👤MU Housing

The MU Housing Healthy Communities Committee is partnering with Tiger Pantry for More Than a Meal, a campus-wide food drive taking place November 5–19 in all MU Housing residence halls and apartments! In recognition of National Hunger and Homelessness Awareness Week, we’re collecting non-perishable food items to help support students, faculty, and staff who may need access to food resources. 🥫 How to Participate Drop off non-perishable food items in your building's main lobby from November 5–19th.
