Boone County History Makers Hall of Fame Gala 2026

🗓️Sat, Oct 24 · 5:30 PM – 9 PM CDT
📍Country Club of Missouri, 3801 Ponderosa St Columbia, MO 65201
👤Visit Columbia

The Boone County Historical Society’s Endowment Trust Board is proud to announce the 2026 enshrinees into the Boone County Hall of Fame: Carl Edwards, Wendy Noren (posthumous), and the Boone County Community Trust. These distinguished honorees will be formally enshrined at the annual Boone County History Makers Hall of Fame Gala on Saturday, October 24, 2026, at the Country Club of Missouri.
