Rising Innovators: Rural News

🗓️Thu, Nov 5 · 4 PM – 5 PM CST
📍Reynolds Journalism Institute
🎟️Free

Aidan Cornue, Editor in Chief of the Oxford Free Press in Ohio, is the next guest speaker for our Rising Innovators series! He will share his journey into rural news, the challenge of news deserts and growing a newsroom from the ground up. He will also discuss the daily tasks for running a weekly newspaper, as well as the importance of community involvement both in and out of work.
