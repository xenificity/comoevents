Week of Writing Faculty Panel–From Professional to Professor: Building your Bridge into Academia

🗓️Wed, Oct 21 · 4 PM – 5 PM CDT
📍Ellis Library, 1020 Lowry Mall, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Provost

The Week Of Writing is a weeklong celebration of writing in all of its forms, stages, uses, functions, approaches, and levels of joy (and challenge). We are interested in hearing how undergraduate students, graduate students, post-docs, faculty and staff use, grapple with and/or celebrate writing across campus. From the Humanities to STEM, in any language or form, writing is an act that unites us all in academia (and maybe as people?). The WOW is our time to acknowledge, learn about, and celebrate it all.
