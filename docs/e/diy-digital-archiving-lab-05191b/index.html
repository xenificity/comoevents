DIY Digital Archiving Lab

🗓️Wed, Dec 2 · 11 AM – 11:45 AM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

Bring your photos, documents, artwork, slides and negatives to this weekly session to digitize them using our up-to-date scanning equipment. Orientation to the equipment and assistance from staff is available during this lab time. Bring a USB drive or a login for a cloud storage account. Adults and teens. Register for December sessions starting November 23, one person per session.
