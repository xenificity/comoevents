Trivia Night with Let’s Get Trivial

🗓️Tue, Dec 1 · 6 PM – 8 PM CST
📍Cooper's Landing, 11505 Smith Hatchery Rd, Columbia, MO 65203
👤Cooper's Landing

@ Cooper’s Landing is excited to welcome Trivia Master, Zeb, from Let’s Get Trivial, to host some trivia fun! Trivia goes from 6 – 8 pm, so don’t be late and bring your friends! Trivia can be solo or in teams of any size. You will hear general questions spanning a range of topics from sports, music, movies, current and historical events, people, and places. It’s anyone’s game. Prizes will be given to the 1st and 2nd place winners! Come on down and enjoy the fun!
