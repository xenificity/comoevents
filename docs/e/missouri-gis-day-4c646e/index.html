Missouri GIS Day

🗓️Tue, Nov 17 · 11 AM – 3 PM CST
📍Harry S. Truman Building
🎟️Free
👤Msdis

Celebrate GIS Day on November 17 with Missouri's Office of Geospatial Information (OGI) and the Missouri Spatial Data Information Service (MSDIS). The event will feature presentations from OGI and MSDIS highlighting geospatial resources, initiatives, and applications across Missouri. Afternoon research and poster sessions will showcase how students and researchers use GIS and related technologies to explore questions and address real-world challenges. Join us for a day of learning, sharing ideas, and connecting with Missouri's geospatial community.
