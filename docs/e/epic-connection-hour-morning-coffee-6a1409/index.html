EPIC Connection Hour - Morning Coffee

🗓️Wed, Dec 2 · 7:30 AM – 9 AM CST
📍Acola Coffee Company, 300 N 10th St. Ste 100, Columbia, MO 65201
👤Columbia Chamber of Commerce

EPIC's monthly connection hour is a great way to meet new people and reconnect with old friends. The connection hour will take place the first Wednesday of each month, and will alternate between an afternoon happy hour from 4:00-5:30 pm at Logboat Brewing Company and a morning coffee from 7:30-9:00 am at Acola Coffee Company. It is completely free to attend and buy your own drink. Just look for the tables with EPIC signs on them to know what group to join.
