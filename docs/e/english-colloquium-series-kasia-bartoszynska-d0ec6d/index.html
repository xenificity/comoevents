English Colloquium Series: Kasia Bartoszynska

🗓️Fri, Nov 13 · 3:30 PM – 5:30 PM CST
📍Tate Hall 215
👤English

Join us in welcoming our next colloquium visitor Kasia Bartoszynska of Ithaca College on Friday, November 13th at 3:30pm in Tate Hall Room 215. She will be giving a talk, "Free Indirect Me: Experiments in First-Person Voice in 18th and 21st Century Fiction." Though there is plenty of critical disagreement about what free indirect discourse really is, there would seem to be a broad consensus about what it is not: the first-person voice.
