Beyond the Pink: Stories, Strength & Sisterhood

🗓️Thu, Oct 29 · 5:30 PM – 7 PM CDT
📍Memorial Student Union, The Eyler Room (S110), University of Missouri, Columbia, Missouri, United States
👤Women Economic Empowerment (WEE) @ MIZZOU

Join Women Economic Empowerment (WEE) @ Mizzou, in collaboration with Women of Color in Health Care (WOCHC), for Beyond the Pink: Stories, Strength & Sisterhood, a special gathering in recognition of Breast Cancer Awareness Month! This event is designed to bring students together in a relaxed, welcoming, and interactive environment to raise awareness about breast cancer, celebrate survivors, honor those affected, and encourage meaningful conversations about breast health and the importance of community support.
