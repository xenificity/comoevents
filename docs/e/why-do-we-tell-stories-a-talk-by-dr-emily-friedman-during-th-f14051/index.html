“Why Do We Tell Stories?” A Talk by Dr. Emily Friedman during the Week of Writing

🗓️Fri, Oct 23 · 7 PM CDT
📍Ellis Library, 1020 Lowry Mall, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Provost

The Week of Writing is a weeklong celebration of writing in all of its forms, stages, uses, functions, approaches, and levels of joy (and challenge). We are interested in hearing how undergraduate students, graduate students, post-docs, faculty and staff use, grapple with and/or celebrate writing across campus. From the Humanities to STEM, in any language or form, writing is an act that unites us all in academia (and maybe as people?). The WOW is our time to acknowledge, learn about, and celebrate it all. 2026 Co-Sponsors: Campus Writing Program, Center for the Humanities , Creative Writing…
