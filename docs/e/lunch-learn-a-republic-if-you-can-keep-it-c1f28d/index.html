Lunch & Learn: A Republic, If You Can Keep It

🗓️Wed, Dec 9 · 12 PM – 1 PM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

Dr. Heather Carlquist Walser, assistant professor of history at MU, will explore the Constitution’s original design for a balance of power among the branches of government and consider what shifts in that balance over time mean for representative government today. Co-sponsored by the League of Women Voters. Adults. Attend in person or via Zoom; see full description for details.
