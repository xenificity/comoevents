Columbia Farmers Market – Winter Season

🗓️Sat, Nov 7 · 9 AM – 12 PM CST
📍Winter Season, 1769 West Ash Columbia, MO 65203
👤Visit Columbia

Experience the taste of mid-Missouri year-round at Columbia Farmers Market! Visit us during the Winter Season: every Saturday from 9 a.m.- noon (November-March) in the heated MU Health Care Pavilion at Columbia’s Agriculture Park. You will find fresh produce, meat, farm fresh eggs, cheese, honey, artisan items and more! As a producer-only market, everything sold at CFM is offered by the farmers and artisans who help sustain our region.
