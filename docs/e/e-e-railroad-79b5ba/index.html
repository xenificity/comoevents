E. & E. Railroad

🗓️Sat, Oct 24 · 6 PM – 9 PM CDT
📍Cooper's Landing, 11505 Smith Hatchery Rd, Columbia, MO 65203
👤Cooper's Landing

@ Join us as E. & E. Railroad takes the stage for an unforgettable performance along the river. Bring your friends, grab a drink, and soak up the afternoon with us! E&E Railroad is a mix of old-time and roots music, traditional and modern. The band consists of Ellen Gomez on fiddle, John Ellis on guitar, Mike O’Neill on mandolin, and Vince Corkery on bass. The band features strong vocal harmonies and exciting instrumental arrangements of songs old and new – from fiddle tunes to contemporary songs.
