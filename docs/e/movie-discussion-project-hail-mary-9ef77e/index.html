Movie Discussion: Project Hail Mary

🗓️Thu, Nov 19 · 2 PM – 3 PM CST
📍The Bridge, 220 Townsend Hall, MO
👤College of Education & Human Development

Join us to discuss Project Hail Mary, a sci-fi film following Dr. Ryland Grace, a middle school teacher and molecular biologist. Waking up alone on a spaceship with amnesia, he slowly recalls being sent on a desperate, last-ditch mission to save Earth from a microscopic organism that is draining the sun's energy.
