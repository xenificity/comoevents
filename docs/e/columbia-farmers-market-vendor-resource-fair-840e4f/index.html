Columbia Farmers Market: Vendor Resource Fair

🗓️Tue, Nov 17 · 5:30 PM – 7 PM CST
📍Columbia Farmers Market, 1769 W. Ash Columbia, MO 65203
👤Visit Columbia

Are you a farmer, processor, artisan, or prepared food business operating within 50 miles of the Columbia Farmers Market and interested in becoming a vendor? Join us for a free Vendor Resource Fair on Tuesday, November 17, from 5:30-7:00 PM at the heated MU Health Care Pavilion at Columbia’s Agriculture Park (1769 W. Ash). Learn the requirements for joining our producer-only market and speak one-on-one with local businesses and agencies that can provide information and resources to help you prepare to be a successful CFM vendor.
