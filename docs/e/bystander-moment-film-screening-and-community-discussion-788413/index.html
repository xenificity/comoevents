Bystander Moment Film Screening and Community Discussion

🗓️Thu, Dec 3 · 5:30 PM – 7 PM CST
📍1001 Rogers St, Columbia, MO 65216
👤Columbia College

The Inclusive Pedagogy Committee, in partnership with the Title IX Office, invites you to a screening and community discussion of The Bystander Moment . Recent headlines from college campuses raise an important question: Why do people remain silent when something is wrong, and what helps them take action? The Bystander Moment (2018) explores the barriers to intervention and the role individuals and communities can play in preventing harm. A follow-up event expanding on these themes is planned for Spring 2027.
