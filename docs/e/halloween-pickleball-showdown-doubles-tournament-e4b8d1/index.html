Halloween Pickleball Showdown: Doubles Tournament

🗓️Sat, Oct 24 · 6 PM – 9 PM CDT
📍3711 Discovery Parkway, Columbia, MO
👤Visit Columbia

Get ready for a spooktacular night on the courts! Join us at The Kitchen Grill & Games on October 24th from 6–9 PM for a Halloween-themed pickleball doubles tournament filled with friendly competition, festive fun, delicious bites, and plenty of Halloween spirit! Grab your doubles partner, bring your A-game, and show off your Halloween style! Costumes are encouraged, and we’re celebrating everyone from the most competitive players to those just here for the fun. Prizes will be awarded to the best- and worst-placing teams, plus a best-dressed costume winner selected by our staff!
