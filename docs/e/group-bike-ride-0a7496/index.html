Group Bike Ride

🗓️Thu, Nov 5 · 3:30 PM – 5:30 PM CST
📍General Services Building, 900 E Stadium Blvd, Columbia 65201, United States
👤Sustain Mizzou

Join us for a group bike ride to learn more about Columbia's bike paths, biking resources, and more! We will meet at the parking lot to the right of the General Services Building and head down to the Green Briar Connector. This will be a fun way to get out into nature and enjoy the fall weather, meet new people or chat with old friends, and learn some things about sustainability along the way. From beginning to expert bikers, this trip is open all. Please plan to bring your own bike if you have one. A limited number of bikes (10) will be available to reserve in advance.
