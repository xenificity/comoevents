Bengali Durga Puja 2026

🗓️Sun, Oct 25 · 10 AM – 4 PM CDT
📍Shanthi Mandir, 2006 Holly Ave, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Bengali Association of Mid-Missouri (BAMM)

Durga Puja celebrates the triumph of good over evil and the divine power of Goddess Durga. Program: 10 AM puja · 11:45 AM pushpanjali followed by prasad · 12:15 PM cultural program 1 · 1 PM Bengali khichuri bhog · 2 PM cultural program 2 · 3 PM onwards aarti, sindoor khela and dhunuchi dance. Kids 3–16: bring a drawing on the Durga Pujo theme for the drawing competition; artwork is displayed on puja day. Sponsored by BAMM at the HTCC temple, 2006 Holly Ave.
