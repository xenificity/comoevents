DIY Digital Archiving Lab

🗓️Wed, Nov 11 · 12 PM – 12:45 PM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

Bring your photos, documents, artwork, slides and negatives to this weekly session to digitize them using our up-to-date scanning equipment. Orientation to the equipment and assistance from staff is available during this lab time. Bring a USB drive or a login for a cloud storage account. Adults and teens. Register for November sessions starting October 26, one person per session.
