When Dylan Met Guthrie

🗓️Tue, Nov 10 · 6:30 PM – 7:30 PM CST
📍Columbia Public Library, 100 W Broadway, Columbia, MO
🎟️Free
👤Daniel Boone Regional Library

Award-winning, crowd-pleasing musicians Lil Rev and Will Branch weave together a unique blend of stories, quotes, poetry and classic Woody Guthrie and Bob Dylan songs for this show, including many sing-along favorites. Never content to imitate, Will and Rev possess a delightful enthusiasm for the material. Enjoy their refreshing renditions of these timeless tunes! Adults.
