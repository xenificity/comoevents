Mizzou Women's Basketball vs Saint Louis

🗓️Sun, Dec 13 · 1 PM – 4 PM CST
📍Mizzou Arena, 1 Champions Dr, Columbia, MO
👤Mizzou Athletics

Join us at Mizzou Arena as the Mizzou Women's Basketball team tips off against in-state foe Saint Louis on Residence Hall Association Day and Alumni Day! Enjoy free ice cream and a scarf giveaway for Mizzou students courtesy of the Mizzou Residence Hall Association, help us welcome back our Mizzou Women's Basketball alumni, and cheer on the Saint Louis Disc Dogs at halftime! Tipoff is scheduled for 1:00 PM. Admission is free for all Mizzou students! Check in at the game on the Mizzou Tigers App to earn points towards Men's Basketball tickets!
