Smash & Dash

🗓️Tue, Dec 1 · 4:30 PM – 8 PM CST
📍Cooper's Landing, 11505 Smith Hatchery Rd, Columbia, MO 65203
👤Cooper's Landing

@ Smash & Dash is one of Columbia’s newest Food Trucks on the scene! They are most known for their signature burger: The One Night Stand. This mouth-watering burger has their homemade chili on top of two patties. Topped with 3 slices of bacon, 2 slices of American cheese, freshly sliced jalapenos, onions, sour cream, and nacho cheese, truly makes this burger the sloppiest ‘One Night Stand’ you’ve EVER had! And if you are into going slow and starting off with a more traditional burger or any of the delicious sides, that is perfectly acceptable! Make it a date with Smash & Dash!
