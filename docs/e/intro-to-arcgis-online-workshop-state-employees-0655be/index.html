Intro to ArcGIS Online Workshop - State Employees

🗓️Wed, Oct 28 · 8 AM – 12 PM CDT
📍Strickland Hall, Strickland Hall, University of Missouri, Columbia, MO
👤Msdis

Introduction to ArcGIS Online is a hands-on workshop designed specifically for Missouri state employees who are new to ArcGIS Online or looking to strengthen their understanding of web-based GIS tools and workflows. This training provides practical experience with Esri's cloud-based GIS environment and introduces the core concepts used to create, manage, analyze, and share geographic information online.
