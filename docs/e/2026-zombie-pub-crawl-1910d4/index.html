2026 Zombie Pub Crawl

🗓️Sat, Oct 31 · 6 PM – 11 PM CDT
📍Columbia, MO
👤Visit Columbia

The zombies are taking over Downtown Columbia this Halloween! Join us for the Woodhaven Zombie Pub Crawl on October 31 for a night of costumes, cocktails, spooky fun and supporting a great cause. Your $15 wristband gets you free cover at 20+ participating downtown bars along with exclusive food and drink specials throughout the night. Best of all, proceeds from the event support Woodhaven’s programs and services for adults with developmental disabilities and neurodivergent individuals in Columbia, MO.
