Forager One: Best Practices to Connect with a Mentor (5 November 2026) (S.T.A.R. eligible)

🗓️Thu, Nov 5 · 4 PM – 5 PM CST
📍Ellis Library, Room 4D11 (computer classroom), University of Missouri, Columbia, Missouri, United States
👤Undergraduate Research

Are you looking for a faculty mentor or research opportunities on campus? Attend this session to learn how to use ForagerOne to connect with Mizzou faculty researchers and other students for collaborative projects. You’ll learn helpful tips to make a positive first impression with your profile, connection request, and initial meeting. Join the Zoom meeting from a Mizzou-authenticated account. Participation in this presentation will be applied to the S.T.A.R. recognition if attendees 1) are present for at least 40 minutes of the presentation and 2) join breakout room discussions.
