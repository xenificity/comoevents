5th Annual Merry Moments with Santa & Mrs. Claus

🗓️Fri, Nov 20 · 5 PM – 8 PM CST
📍106 Orr St Columbia, MO 65201
👤Visit Columbia

Celebrate the holiday season with Junior Achievement in Central Missouri at the 5th Annual Merry Moments with Santa & Mrs. Claus! Families are invited to enjoy a free evening of holiday fun featuring photos with Santa and Mrs. Claus, cookies and cocoa, holiday crafts and games, letters to Santa, and family activities and resources from local community organizations. The event will be held from 5:00–8:00 p.m. on Friday, November 20, at Orr Street Studios in downtown Columbia. Admission is completely free, and no registration is required—families can simply stop by and join the celebration!
