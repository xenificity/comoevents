Venues to visit

Places in Columbia that hold events but don't list them anywhere the app can read. Visit them, show them the app, and ask them to post their events.

What to say

"Hi, I made CoMo Events, a free map of everything happening in Columbia. Your events aren't on it because they're only on Facebook. You can post them free in under a minute: open comoevent.com, tap Post an event, add the title, time and your venue name, and we fill in the rest. Or send me your calendar link and I'll add it automatically."

Leave a 4x6 card with the QR codes. Ask who handles their events, and get an email or calendar link if they have one.

Checkmarks and notes are saved only in this browser on this device. The list is updated in the app's repository (feed/outreach.json).
