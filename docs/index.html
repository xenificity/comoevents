CoMo Events

Every public event in Columbia, Missouri on one map. Mizzou, downtown, music, parties, markets, the library, family things, all in one place, refreshed around the clock.

Support

Questions, a wrong pin, or an event source we should add? Email xenificity@icloud.com or open an issue at github.com/xenificity/comoevents.

Where the events come from

Public listings from the University of Missouri calendar, Visit Columbia, The Blue Note and Rose Music Hall, Daniel Boone Regional Library, Cooper's Landing, MyHouse (via Posh), Eventbrite, AllEvents, Base Local and Vox Magazine's community calendar. Each event links back to its source for tickets and details.
