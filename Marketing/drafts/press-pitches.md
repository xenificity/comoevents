## The Maneater

**Subject:** Mizzou grad student builds free app that maps every event in Columbia

Hello,

I am a graduate student at Mizzou, and I built a free iPhone app called CoMo Events. It puts every public event in Columbia on one live map: the Mizzou calendar, downtown venues like The Blue Note, the library, student org events and more, refreshed every two hours.

Students told me they only hear about events if they already follow the right accounts. The app fixes that. Student organizations can also post their own events, and a person reviews each one before it goes up.

It has no ads, collects no data, and needs no account. I would be glad to talk about how I built it or show it in person.

## Vox Magazine

**Subject:** A new way to find what is happening in CoMo this weekend

Hello,

I built CoMo Events, a free iPhone app that shows everything happening in Columbia on one map. It pulls from ten public sources, including the Mizzou calendar, Visit Columbia, The Blue Note, Rose Music Hall, Cooper's Landing and Vox's own community calendar, and links back to each source.

You can filter by tonight, this weekend, or by who you are going with: singles, couples, groups or family. There is a party filter, a music filter, and reminders before saved events.

I think it could fit a "things to do" or local tech piece. Happy to share data on what is on in Columbia this month, or to walk you through the app.
