# Student organization outreach

Goal: 30 organizations each share the app once with their members. Start with groups you know
(Indian Student Association, Cultural Association of India, GPC, your department's grad group),
then work through MizzouEngage. Use the `orgs` link so you can count the results later.

---

## Email to an org president or events chair

**Subject:** Free way to get more people to your events

Hello [Name],

I am a Mizzou graduate student, and I built a free iPhone app called CoMo Events. It shows every public event in Columbia on one map, and student organizations can post their events there for free.

When you post an event, it shows up on the map for everyone using the app, not only your members. A person reviews each post, usually within a day.

Two small asks:
1. Post your next public event from the app (the plus button on the Upcoming tab).
2. Share the app once with your members, if you think it is useful.

Get the app: https://xenificity.github.io/comoevents/get/?c=orgs

If it would help, I am happy to come to a meeting for five minutes and show how it works.

Thank you,
Ajay Kumar
[Department], University of Missouri

## GroupMe / Discord message for members (send to the org lead to forward)

Looking for something to do this weekend? CoMo Events is a free app by a Mizzou student that shows everything happening in Columbia on one map: parties, concerts, food, sports and campus events. You can see what is on tonight and save events to get a reminder. Free, no ads: https://xenificity.github.io/comoevents/get/?c=orgs

---

## Five-minute meeting talk (notes)

- Show the map with the Weekend filter on. Tap a busy cluster.
- Show the party and music filters, and "Who is it for".
- Post a sample event live from the plus button.
- Put the QR from `Marketing/qr/qr-orgs.png` on the screen and give people 30 seconds to scan.
