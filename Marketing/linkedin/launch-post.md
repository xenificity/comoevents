## Post (from the CoMo Events page)

Columbia has a lot going on. Finding it should not take ten websites.

That is why we built CoMo Events: a free map of every public event in Columbia, Missouri.

Mizzou talks and games, live music downtown, farmers markets, church and temple festivals, library story times, races and trivia nights. CoMo Events reads more than 30 public calendars, cleans them up and puts them on one live map that refreshes every two hours. Right now that is over 1,300 events.

What you can do with it:
- See what is happening right now, tonight or this weekend
- Filter for singles, couples, groups or families
- Search in plain words, like "live jazz" or "kids this weekend"
- Save events and get a reminder before they start
- Post your own event for free, even from a photo of the flyer

It is free, with no ads.
iPhone app: https://apps.apple.com/app/como-events/id6824363992
Website: https://comoevent.com

If you run events in Columbia, we would love to have them on the map. Post them in the app, or message this page.
